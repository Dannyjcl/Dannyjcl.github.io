Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Daniel's Morning Brief is a personal automation run by Daniel Lee on his own computer using self-hosted n8n. It is used only by its owner.

What the app accesses

The app uses the Gmail API only to send emails from the owner's Gmail account to the owner's own inbox. It does not read, store, share or sell email content or any other Google user data.

Data storage

The OAuth credentials are stored locally in the owner's n8n instance. No data is sent to third parties apart from public market-data APIs, and those requests contain no personal information.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
